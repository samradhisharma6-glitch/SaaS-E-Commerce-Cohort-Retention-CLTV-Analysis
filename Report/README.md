# Executive Summary



This project analyzes customer retention behavior using Cohort Analysis and estimates Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) from retail transaction data. The objective is to understand customer churn patterns, identify profitable customer segments, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving customer retention and long-term business growth.



## Business Problem

Acquiring new customers is significantly more expensive than retaining existing ones. Businesses require clear visibility into customer retention and lifetime value to improve profitability. This project identifies retention trends, customer churn, and high-value customer segments using cohort analysis and CLTV metrics.

#### 

#### Key Findings

* Customer retention decreases over time after acquisition.
* High-value customers generate the majority of business revenue.
* Cohort analysis reveals differences in customer retention across acquisition periods.
* Revenue contribution is concentrated among a relatively small percentage of customers.
* Customer segmentation helps prioritize retention strategies.
